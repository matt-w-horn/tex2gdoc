\section{Introduction}
Arrival rate $\lambda$ and capacity $C$ decide whether a queue settles, as
Figure~\ref{fig:throughput} shows and Table~\ref{tab:cases} summarises.
The condition is $\lambda K < C$~\cite{example2020}.
